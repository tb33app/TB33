TB 33 — About / Rules

Full-date calculation

Add every digit of YEAR + MONTH + DAY. That total is RAW_SUM. Example: 17 September 2001 → 2+0+0+1+0+9+1+7 = 20.

Reduction

Other compound values reduce repeatedly by digit sum. 11, 22 and 33 are preserved. Values such as 44, 55 or 66 are not preserved (44 → 8).

Special 20 → 11

A RAW_SUM of exactly 20 becomes 11 with the note “hidden 11 (20)”. 20 must never reduce to 2. Other totals that reduce to 11 (for example 29) show “master 11 (from 29)”.

Full 22 / impure 22

If the final full-date value is 22, it is full/master 22 when neither the month nor the day is a reducible compound (>9 and not 11/22/33). If the month or day is a reducible compound it shows 22/4 and is labelled impure 22. The Gregorian year never participates in this check.

Full 33 / impure 33

Likewise 33 shows as 33 (full/master 33) or 33/6 (impure 33) using the same month/day-only check.

Chinese zodiac boundaries

Animals are assigned by exact date using Chinese New Year boundaries, matched with START ≤ date ≤ END. A date is not assigned an animal from its Gregorian year alone. Example: 15 January 2000 is CAT because Chinese New Year 2000 had not yet occurred; 5 February 2000 is DRAGON.

CAT naming convention

The conventional Rabbit label is displayed as CAT throughout TB 33. This is a label change only; the underlying Chinese calendar dates are unchanged (not the Vietnamese calendar).

Supported ranges

  • Calendar and Energy: 1 January 1800 – 31 December 2200.
  • Chinese Year: Gregorian years 1527 – 2526.

Long-range Chinese dates

The Chinese-year table is a historical reconstruction plus future projection. Far-future boundaries can be model-sensitive. TB 33 keeps one internally consistent main dataset and flags the known model-sensitive boundaries around 2261, 2372, 2375 and 2498.

Note

Numerology is a symbolic system. It is not a scientifically established predictive measurement. TB 33 performs arithmetic and calendar lookup only; it makes no claims about events, personality or outcomes.
