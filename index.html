Appearance and calculation rules

Theme

Secondary Energy

Secondary Energy uses the existing TB33 day/birthday component: the calendar day reduced with the same 11/22/33 preservation and special 20 → 11 rule. Example: day 24 → 2+4 → 6.

Impure 33 / 33÷6

TB33 keeps the full-date calculation and also checks both component routes for impure 33: reduced day + reduced month + reduced year number, and whole/unreduced calendar day + reduced month + reduced year number. A component-path 33 is displayed as 33/6.

Master highlighting

11, 22 and 33-family results are visually highlighted in the calendar. The Orange theme switches master highlighting to purple so special dates remain distinct from the theme accent.

Western astrology

Western signs use the standard tropical Sun-sign date ranges. No birth time or location is used.

Chinese astrology

Chinese animals use the existing exact Chinese New Year boundaries. TB33 displays the conventional Rabbit label as CAT; this is a naming convention only.
